# Outsoul-chan character design

## Reference analysis

All 11 files in imagens de base/ were inspected together in a contact sheet. The recurring identity is lavender-pink layered hair with a prominent curl, magenta eyes, a dark broken-heart clip, cross-shaped hair pins, black oversized goth-kawaii clothing, pink/purple trim, and heart jewelry. Expressions range from smug and sleepy to excited, angry, crying, and startled.

## Design anchors

- **Silhouette:** petite adult anime woman; oversized dark jacket, layered hair and a visible crown curl.
- **Face:** rounded adult face, expressive brows and mouth, pink/magenta eyes.
- **Hair:** lavender base with pale pink highlights; messy layered volume.
- **Clothes:** black and violet goth-kawaii streetwear, oversized sleeves, pink trim.
- **Accessories:** black broken-heart clip, cross pins, heart necklace; optional small character hair clip.
- **Palette:** lavender #C99AE8, pale lilac #F0D7FF, magenta #E43FA6, black #17131C, violet #703A91.

## Invariants

Keep the lilac hair, magenta eyes, black broken-heart clip, and dark/pink goth-kawaii palette. Her personality is a Brazilian shitposter; her appearance does not imply yandere behavior.

## Allowed variation

Chibi distortion, expressive poses, cinematic lighting, manga hatching, framing, facial expression, and clothing folds may vary while the character remains recognizable.

## Prohibited variation

Do not make her look like a child, remove all identity anchors, add romantic/possessive characterization, add text or watermarks, reproduce another character, or copy one reference illustration's composition.
