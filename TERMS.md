# Terms of use — Outsoul-chan

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Outsoul-chan is an experimental automated character operated through Reddit's Developer Platform. It is not an official Reddit service and does not provide professional, medical, legal, financial, or emergency advice.

## Using the app

The app may generate inaccurate, incomplete, delayed, or unexpected replies and may be unavailable. Replies are generated from the Reddit post/comment context available to the app and, when enabled, an AI provider. Do not rely on a reply as authoritative information.

Use the app in accordance with Reddit's [User Agreement](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/user-agreement), [Content Policy](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/content-policy), and the rules of the subreddit where it is installed. Do not include passwords, API keys, private contact details, or other sensitive information in a public post or comment addressed to the app.

Reddit posts and comments are public according to Reddit's features and settings. The app's responses are also public Reddit comments. Reddit's and the community moderators' normal rules and moderation tools apply to all content.

## AI processing and availability

When Gemini is enabled, relevant Reddit text, limited conversation context, and (for image posts) Reddit-hosted image data may be sent to Google's Gemini API to produce a response. Details about the app's storage and processing are in the [Privacy Notice](https://github.com/brunomello/outsoul-chan/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

Subreddit moderators may change settings, disable replies, or remove the app. The app's behavior and features may change as it is developed.

## Contact

For questions about the app, contact the moderators of [r/japan_outsoul](https://www.reddit.com/r/japan_outsoul/).
